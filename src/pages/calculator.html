קידוש החודש Rambam · KH 11–17

Every number here is computed on this page by the project's own engine (MIT), bundled verbatim from shluchimexchange.ai/kh/engine and checked step-for-step against its live API. ליל ל׳ is located the Rambam's own way: day 1 of a month is the first evening after the molad on which this calculation says the crescent showed, and the thirtieth night falls 29 days later. A real court took day 1 from witnesses. The four galgalim behind all of this are drawn, chapter by chapter, here. These are the 26 astronomical steps; the separate fixed-calendar chain (molad / BaHaRaD, KH 6–10) lives on the full dashboard. Teaching notes in the Rabbi Losh tradition, via Rabbi Zajac's classes — credit him and Chabad.org when quoting.
